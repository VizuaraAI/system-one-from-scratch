Built today

What we built

The serving code is public, but the training scripts are not. We recovered the recipe from the authors’ checkpoints, wrote our own trainer, and ran every stage ourselves.

1. Vectors625 chunks of frozen Qwen3-8B vectors, re-checked at cosine 0.9999+
2. Pre-training62M pairs, 30,000 steps, about 1.7 hours on one GPU
3. Mid-trainingUp to 10 tricky wrong answers per question, on 2.8M pairs
4. Post-training847k agent decisions from 35 datasets, embedded in about an hour
5. ServingThe authors’ engine and cache running our weights
6. Fine-tuningDeepSWE verifier in about 12 seconds

The idea

How it works

Turn every state and every action into a vector. Train so a state lands close to the action that was taken. At runtime, the closest action is the decision.

Architecture: one frozen encoder, two small heads

Question + document→Qwen3-8B, frozen→State head, 9.44M→512-d
Answer→the same Qwen3-8B→Action head, 9.44M→512-d
